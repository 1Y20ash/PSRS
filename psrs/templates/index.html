Plain-English guide

A few useful words

Forecast error +

How far the estimate was from the actual sample value. A smaller error usually means a better estimate.

Stock check +

Estimating current stock from starting amounts, received items, and items sent out.

Possible route +

A path that connects the chosen starting point to the destination in this sample network.

Sample data +

Made-up records used to demonstrate the project. They do not describe real locations or real supply needs.
