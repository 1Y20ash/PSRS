Quick glossary

Words you will see in PSRS

MAE +

Mean Absolute Error: the average size of forecast mistakes. Lower is generally better.

Reconciliation +

Rebuilding an estimated stock position from opening balances, receipts, and issues.

Feasible route +

A route that exists in the fictional road graph. PSRS flags destinations it cannot reach.

Ground truth +

Hidden future values used only after a forecast is produced to measure performance fairly.

PSRS research prototype All values are synthetic and fictional · Human review required
